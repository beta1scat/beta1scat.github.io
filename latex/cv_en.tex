\section*{Profile}
Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology, specializing in robotic perception, motion planning, and robust grasp synthesis for complex, unstructured manipulation scenarios. Research integrates 3D vision, geometric reasoning, analytical kinematics, hand-eye/tool calibration, and language-guided grasping into closed-loop robotic systems. Complements analytical kinematics and geometric priors with data-driven paradigms, actively investigating Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and reinforcement learning for embodied manipulation. Solid engineering background in C/C++, Python, PyTorch, and ROS, with extensive experience deploying dual-arm, mobile, and industrial robotic systems.

\section*{Contribution Overview}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Proposed a general analytical inverse kinematics framework that enables industrial robots with the same configuration to share one D-H-parameter-based solver.
	\item Introduced a joint calibration model for hand-eye transformation and tool coordinates to reduce error propagation in robotic grasping.
	\item Built a parameter optimization framework for point-cloud pose estimation to reduce repetitive manual tuning for different objects.
	\item Developed a scene understanding pipeline that combines segmentation, point-cloud classification, and geometric primitive fitting for interpretable perception.
	\item Proposed a language-guided model-free grasping method that integrates multimodal localization, primitive fitting, and task-oriented grasp selection.
	\item Analyzed dynamic vertical climbing in domestic cats with a linear inverted pendulum model and clarified the key mechanism behind dynamic wall traversal without static adhesion.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Academic Projects}
\entry{Humanoid Motion Coordination for a Dual-Arm Robot}{Sep. 2017 -- Jun. 2020}{Core member}{Academic project}
Designed a cable-driven seven-DOF manipulator, established its D-H-based kinematic model, proposed an inverse kinematics algorithm for humanoid motion, and developed Cartesian-space and joint-space planning methods for shaft-hole assembly tasks.

\entry{Biomimetic Quadruped Robot with Integrated Joint Actuation}{Sep. 2020 -- Aug. 2022}{Core member}{Academic project}
Developed insect-inspired integrated joint actuators and investigated feline vertical dynamic climbing mechanisms. Acquired motion sequences via high-speed imaging, analyzed contact dynamics and impact dissipation via the SLIP model, and designed an elastic actuator prototype featuring active compliance and passive restraint.

\entry{Object Perception and Motion Planning for Robotic Grasping}{Aug. 2022 -- Aug. 2024}{Core member}{Academic project}
Led the algorithmic pipeline for unstructured bin picking and cluttered grasping: developed multi-DOF robot kinematics, collision-free trajectory planning, robust hand-eye calibration, point-cloud pose estimation, and contact-stable grasp pose generation with geometric filtering.
